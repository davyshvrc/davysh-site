The process

  1. 01

    Brief

    Send the request form: references, platform, rights, deadline, budget.

  2. 02

    Quote

    You get a fixed scope and a fixed price. Follow-up happens on Discord.

  3. 03

    Deposit

    50% secures your slot. Work starts when it clears.

  4. 04

    Build

    You review at blockout and again at detail. Revisions are counted per tier.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Balance paid, files released, setup guide included.

Terms

  • All prices are starting points. Your quote is final once accepted.
  • 50% deposit to begin. Deposits are non-refundable. Work starts on payment.
  • All payments are final. No refunds.
  • The balance is due before final files are released.
  • Exclusive: the piece is never sold or reused by me. Shared rights: I may also sell it in my store later.
  • Personal use by default. Reselling or redistributing the files requires the commercial rights add-on.
  • Paid bases and third-party assets are bought by the client and must be licensed for the use.
  • I may show finished work in my portfolio unless you choose Private.
  • I never log into your accounts. You upload, with a setup guide from me.
  • No recreations of characters, brands or levels you do not hold the rights to. Everything follows VRChat’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Projects with no reply from the client for 30 days are closed. The deposit is kept.
